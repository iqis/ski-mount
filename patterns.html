Hole patterns

Every hole is a coordinate in millimetres, measured from a boot reference: the boot toe for sole bindings, the 3-pin line for 75 mm telemark, the mount line for plates. Positive is toward the tip.

63 of the 118 are marked shape only: the source shows the hole spacing but not where the pattern sits relative to the boot, so those are drawn without a reference line — set the position yourself in the designer.

Jigarex plate checklist
How it works
Each setup is one binding on one boot. Alpine, AT and NTN boots are placed by BSL (toe = center − BSL/2); 75 mm tele boots by heel-to-pin-line length L (pin line = center − L/2). Center and every along value follow the mounting convention: + is toward the tip, − toward the tail, measured from the ski’s recommended boot center. Positions step the whole setup fore and aft.
Floating heel pieces
Two telemark heel pieces — the Voilé heel pad and the 22 Designs Axl climber — aren’t position-critical. With “heel piece floats” on, the designer places each one anywhere within ± the tolerance of where it belongs: first to keep every insert clear of the warn line, then to share existing inserts, then as close to nominal as possible. One placement serves every ladder position it can reach, so a 60 mm ladder needs two or three heel placements instead of four. The Bishop BMF heel bracket, alpine heels, NTN rear plates and tech heels stay fixed.
Trust tags
Cross-checked: two or more independent templates agree. Measured: one maker or template author, read from the vector file. Single source: one secondary library. Sources disagree: offsets differ; the setup’s notes say by how much. Derived: assembled from related patterns. Shape only: hole spacing without a position — set its shift under More.
Conflicts found
Strive 12/14 heel: Powderguide and SlideWright put the front row 16 mm ahead of the heel; the binding-templates library says 28. Look Pivot/PX toe: 6.5 mm between sources. Marker Jester toe: 2 mm. 22 Designs pin lines: 1.2–1.6 mm. Sliding heels (MTN, Attack) up to 10 mm, inside their adjustment.
Sources
Voilé and 22 Designs templates; Powderguide templates by Knut Pohl; SlideWright; EarnYourTurns; github.com/tomahawkins/binding-templates; skimo.co hole pattern table; Binding Freedom’s Jigarex plate list. Each setup links its own sources.
Spacing
Binding Freedom inserts are about 7.85 mm across. No maker publishes a minimum spacing; 20 mm center to center is the usual rule and the tightest precedent found is just over 14 mm. Shared inserts only work if the binding’s screw holes allow that much play.
Before drilling
Tape a 1:1 print or the binding itself to the ski and check every hole. This page plans; it does not replace a jig.
